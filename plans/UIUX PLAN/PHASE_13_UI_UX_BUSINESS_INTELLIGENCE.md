# Phase 13 — Business Intelligence UX

## Objective
Turn operational data into restrained, decision-oriented reporting.

## Implement
- Revenue summary.
- P&L.
- Attendance rate.
- Payment outstanding.
- Ticket/certificate delivery metrics.
- Funnel conversion metrics.
- Event comparison.
- Export/report actions.

## Visual Rule
Prefer a few meaningful charts and clear tables over a wall of widgets.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Executive can understand financial and operational health quickly.
- Filters are consistent across reports.
- Every KPI has context/time/event scope.
